## Key Takeaways
- **Semantic role understanding partially emerges from pre-training alone** — Frozen decoder-only transformers probed for semantic roles ("who did what to whom") show substantial performance, indicating this knowledge is encoded during pre-training without task-specific fine-tuning.
- **Fine-tuning still improves performance** — While pre-trained representations contain significant semantic role information, fine-tuned models outperform frozen + probing approaches, suggesting incomplete emergence.
- **Emergence scales with model size** — As model scale increases, the internal representation of semantic role structure shifts toward more distributed representations, and probing performance on frozen representations improves.
