## Key Takeaways

**(a) Core Problem:** Polarimetric imaging with Division-of-Focal-Plane (DoFP) cameras faces two major challenges: (1) recovering polarization parameters (DoLP, AoP) from mosaic arrays is a difficult inverse problem, and (2) hardware bottlenecks prevent high-frame-rate acquisition, limiting dynamic video applications. No prior work addressed joint spatial-temporal enhancement for polarization video.

**(b) Method/Mechanism:** PolarVSR proposes the first space-time polarization video reconstruction architecture. It jointly models polarization directions across space and time using a polarization-aware implicit neural representation (INR) for continuous, high-fidelity upsampling. A flow-guided polarization variation loss supervises temporal polarization dynamics by analyzing changes in polarization parameters over time.

**(c) Results:** The paper establishes the first large-scale color DoFP polarization video benchmark. Extensive experiments on this benchmark demonstrate the effectiveness of the method for continuous space-time polarization video reconstruction, though specific quantitative metrics are not detailed in the abstract.
