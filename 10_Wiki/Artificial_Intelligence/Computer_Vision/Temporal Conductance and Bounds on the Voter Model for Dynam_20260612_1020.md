# Temporal Conductance and Bounds on the Voter Model for Dynamic Networks

> **Source:** [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2606.13374v1)
> **Category:** Artificial_Intelligence/Computer_Vision
> **Collected:** 20260612_1020

## Summary

The voter model is a classical stochastic process that models how opinions might spread through a network: at each step, every node lazily adopts the opinion of a random neighbour; eventually all nodes share the same opinion (consensus). Stronger connectivity should yield faster consensus. Berenbrin
