# OrthoReg: Orthogonal Regularization for Hybrid Symbolic-Neural Dynamical Systems

> **Source:** [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2606.19145v1)
> **Category:** Artificial_Intelligence/Reinforcement_Learning
> **Collected:** 20260618_1220

## Summary

Dynamical systems are fundamental to modeling the natural world, yet modeling them involves a persistent trade-off: manually prescribed mechanistic models are interpretable by design but often overly simplistic and misspecified; in contrast, flexible data-driven neural methods lack physical insight.
