# On dynamic multi-agent pathfinding methods: review, simulations and modifications

> **Source:** [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2606.03735v1)
> **Category:** Artificial_Intelligence/AI_Agents
> **Collected:** 20260604_0221

## Summary

This paper presents a systematic study of pathfinding algorithms in the context of Dynamic Multi-Agent Pathfinding (D-MAPF), a setting that combines dynamic obstacles, partial observability, and inter-agent conflicts. We evaluate six representative algorithms: Dijkstra, D* Lite, Space-Time A*, WHCA*
