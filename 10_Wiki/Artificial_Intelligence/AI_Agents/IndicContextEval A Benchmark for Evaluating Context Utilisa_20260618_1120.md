# IndicContextEval: A Benchmark for Evaluating Context Utilisation in Audio Large Language Models Across 8 Indic Languages

> **Source:** [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2606.19157v1)
> **Category:** Artificial_Intelligence/AI_Agents
> **Collected:** 20260618_1120

## Summary

AudioLLMs enable speech recognition conditioned on textual prompts such as domain descriptions or entity lists. However, it remains unclear whether these models genuinely utilise such context or rely on parametric knowledge learned during pretraining. Existing benchmarks cannot answer this question
