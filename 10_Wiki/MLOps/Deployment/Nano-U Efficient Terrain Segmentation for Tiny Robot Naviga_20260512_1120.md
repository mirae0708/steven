# Nano-U: Efficient Terrain Segmentation for Tiny Robot Navigation

> **Source:** [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2605.10210v1)
> **Category:** MLOps/Deployment
> **Collected:** 20260512_1120

## Summary

Terrain segmentation is a fundamental capability for autonomous mobile robots operating in unstructured outdoor environments. However, state-of-the-art models are incompatible with the memory and compute constraints typical of microcontrollers, limiting scalable deployment in small robotics platform
