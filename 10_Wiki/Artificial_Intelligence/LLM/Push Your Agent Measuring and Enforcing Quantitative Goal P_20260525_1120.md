# Push Your Agent: Measuring and Enforcing Quantitative Goal Persistence in Long-Horizon LLM Agents

> **Source:** [arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2605.23574v1)
> **Category:** Artificial_Intelligence/LLM
> **Collected:** 20260525_1120

## Summary

Long-horizon language agents can make many plausible local tool calls yet fail to persist until a requested count is actually complete. We study this gap as Quantitative Goal Persistence (QGP): whether an agent keeps working until an external verifier confirms enough distinct valid items. PushBench
